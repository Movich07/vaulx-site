✦ Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions

Why should I choose VAULX?+

One builder, one point of contact: design and development come from the same hand, with direct communication and no relay through a sales layer.

What services do you offer?+

Website design and development, web and mobile applications, UI/UX design, branding and graphic design (including business logo creation), and ongoing maintenance and IT support.

How much does a project cost?+

It depends on scope, features, and complexity. After an initial consult, you'll get a clear proposal with pricing, and VAULX works with a range of budgets.

How long does a project take?+

A basic website typically takes 3–5 weeks. Larger applications or full brand builds take longer, scoped during consult.

What happens after the project is completed?+

Maintenance and IT support is one of VAULX's core services, not an afterthought, and the relationship continues after launch.
